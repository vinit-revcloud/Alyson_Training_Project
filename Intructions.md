# Paper-only workflow (HR):
1. Interviews → Schedule 

2. Set Assessment delivery to Paper only

3. Fill candidate details and assessment → Schedule paper session (no magic link email is sent)

4. Click Manage on the session

5. Open the Paper test tab (opens by default for paper-only sessions)

6. Upload paper photos → Grade paper test with AI

7. Review Profile report, AI evaluation, and Audit tabs (status becomes evaluated)
